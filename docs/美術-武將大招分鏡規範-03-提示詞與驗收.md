## 6. 提示詞範本（可複製）

先出第 1 張，確認臉與服裝對上立繪後，再出第 2、第 3 張，並把已通過的圖當作風格與連貫參考。

```
You are drawing one still frame of a Three Kingdoms manga storyboard cut-in.
Output: a single 1920x1080 PNG, 16:9, ink-and-watercolor comic style on a light rice-paper background, no border, no text, no watermark, no logo, no panel number.

Character: {GENERAL_NAME} ({GENERAL_ID}).
Must match the official portrait (立繪) exactly: face, beard, hair, headwear, armor, cloak, colors, and weapon.
Weapon: {WEAPON}.
Theme color: {THEME_COLOR}.
Signature beast/effect (mainly for the burst panel, only hinted earlier): {BEAST}.

This is panel {PANEL_INDEX} of {PANEL_COUNT} in ONE continuous move (not alternate poses).
Beat: {BEAT_NAME}. {BEAT_DIRECTION}.
Camera: {CAMERA} shot, same camera side for the whole set: {CAMERA_SIDE}.
Screen direction: the character and weapon travel {FACING} across all panels. Continue the weapon path from the previous panel.
Keep the face and weapon tips inside the safe area: 80 px inset from all edges.
Leave the bottom 200 px clear of the face (banner overlay). On the burst panel, also keep the face out of the right-most third if possible.

Style: clear ink lines, watercolor washes, light paper texture, consistent line weight and palette with the reference images.
No chibi proportions. No photorealism. No typography.

{OPTIONAL_PREVIOUS_PANEL_NOTE}
```

把 `{BEAT_NAME}` 換成 `charge` / `strike` / `burst`（或你的拍號），並加上該拍的具體動作描述。

### 6.1 填好的關羽範例（第 1 張蓄力）

```
You are drawing one still frame of a Three Kingdoms manga storyboard cut-in.
Output: a single 1920x1080 PNG, 16:9, ink-and-watercolor comic style on a light rice-paper background, no border, no text, no watermark, no logo, no panel number.

Character: 關羽 (guan_yu).
Must match the official portrait (立繪) exactly: red face, long black beard, green robe, golden headpiece, dragon-head spaulders, and the Green Dragon Crescent Blade (青龍偃月刀). He is holding the glaive himself; the right hand grips the shaft firmly.
Weapon: 青龍偃月刀, blade long and curved, not a straight spear.
Theme color: deep green robe, gold trim, red face, black beard.
Signature beast/effect (only hinted in this panel): an azure dragon.

This is panel 1 of 3 in ONE continuous move (not alternate poses).
Beat: charge. Guan Yu plants his feet wide, twists his torso, and raises the glaive behind his right shoulder, gathering power. Face is fierce and clear, eyes locked forward.
Camera: medium shot, same camera side for the whole set: viewing his front-left (the glaive is on the right side of the frame, rising backward).
Screen direction: the character and weapon will travel right across all panels. The blade is wound up on the right, ready to sweep or chop toward the right / upper-right.
Keep the face and weapon tips inside the safe area: 80 px inset from all edges.
Leave the bottom 200 px clear of the face (banner overlay).

Style: clear ink lines, watercolor washes, light paper texture, consistent line weight and palette with the official portrait.
No chibi proportions. No photorealism. No typography.
```

第 2 張把 Beat 改成 `strike`：偃月刀揮到身前最高點，綠色氣流與速度線向右，鏡頭略近。第 3 張改成 `burst`：青龍沿刀勢盤出，中遠景，右側與底部留給招式名「青龍斬」（建議）。三張都附立繪，第 2、3 張再附已通過的前一張以維持連貫。

## 7. 驗收清單

出圖後逐張勾選：

- [ ] 每位武將是分開的 PNG，不是一張總表
- [ ] 張數與 `manifest.json` / 播放設定的 `panels` 一致，檔名為 `01_`、`02_`… 連續編號
- [ ] 解析度為 1920×1080、16:9、PNG
- [ ] 無框、無黑邊，紙底畫到邊緣
- [ ] 圖內無文字、無 Logo、無浮水印、無編號
- [ ] 臉、鬍、髮、甲、色、兵器與正式立繪一致，且全套是同一人
- [ ] 畫風、線粗、紙紋、色盤全套一致
- [ ] 全套同一鏡頭側、同一畫面方向，兵器軌跡可接
- [ ] 是同一招的連續瞬間，不是互不相關的姿勢選項
- [ ] 蓄力有表情與預備；出招有速度線；爆發有主題色能量或神獸（若該套有此拍）
- [ ] 臉與刃尖在 80 px 安全區內；底部 200 px 沒有擋住臉；爆發張右側預留疊字空間
- [ ] 不是 Q 版比例，也不是寫實照片風

任一項不合格就重畫該張，不要只做局部塗改而讓色盤或線條跟其他張脫節。

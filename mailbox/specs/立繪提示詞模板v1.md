- 維護：Claude（美術總監）
- 版本日期：2026-10-24 01:05
- 依據：裁決 06 §D 風格要領、裁決 07 女將下限標準

## 使用方式
1. 固定段落（畫風、光影、比例、構圖、輸出）不改字，只替換 `{識別規格卡}` 區塊。
2. 每次產圖附兩張同性別基準圖當風格參考：
   - 男將：趙雲 #22（動態）、關羽 #30（靜態）
   - 女將：大喬 #19（靜態），**只用這一張**；小喬 #35 已降為姿勢參考，不可當風格參考（裁決 07）
3. 每張候選出 3 張，交件附：64px 剪影、灰階縮圖、與基準的並排圖、四角 alpha 檢查。

## 固定段落（英文，直接貼）

```text
STYLE: Eastern fantasy semi-realistic painterly character art, AAA game CG splash quality, thick-paint rendering, no cel-shading, no line art. Match the style of the attached reference image(s) exactly.
LIGHTING: key light from upper left; cool rim light on the shadow side; metal has high-contrast specular reflections; cloth has soft diffuse shading.
DETAIL: ornate gilded engraving on armor following ONE motif theme (given below); beast-face belt buckle; detail concentrated on face, chest and weapon, lower body progressively simpler.
CLOTH: ribbons and cape in the identity main color form the main silhouette; {POSE_CLOTH}.
COLOR: main identity color 60%, metal 30%, accent color 10%; overall low saturation, only the accent is saturated.
COMPOSITION: vertical 2:3, full body in frame, about 3% margin above the head and below the feet; the weapon may exit a corner diagonally but must never crop the hands.
OUTPUT: single character only, fully transparent background, no ground, no shadow plate, no text, no frame, no watermark.
```

`{POSE_CLOTH}` 依版本替換：
- 靜態（轉蛋）：`static heroic standing pose facing the viewer in front three-quarter view, cloth hangs down calmly`
- 動態（人物介紹）：`dynamic battle pose mid-action, cloth swept strongly along the motion direction`

## 比例段落（依體型選一）

```text
[MALE STANDARD] heroic proportions about 8 heads tall, athletic build.
[MALE HEAVY] heroic proportions about 8 heads tall, very broad shoulders about 3 head-widths, powerful heavy build.
[FEMALE] 8 to 8.5 heads tall, mature and graceful; body curves and bust proportion exactly like the attached Da Qiao reference; LIGHT natural makeup only: soft blush, natural lip color, thin eyeliner, no heavy eyeshadow, no heavy contour.
```

## 識別規格卡區塊（每位武將替換）

```text
CHARACTER: {name_en} ({name_zh}).
SILHOUETTE KEY: {剪影特徵，例如 high black ponytail + one short white cape}.
SIGNATURE WEAPON: {招牌武器與握法}.
COLORS: main {主色}, metal {金屬}, accent {點綴色}.
FACE: {臉部特徵：年齡感、眉眼、鬍鬚}.
MOTIF THEME: {紋樣主題，例如 dragon clouds}.
MUST NOT: {禁止事項，例如 no brown hair, no multi-layer cape}.
```

## 範例：趙雲（靜態，裁決 07 修正版）

```text
CHARACTER: Zhao Yun (趙雲).
SILHOUETTE KEY: high black ponytail with blue headband; ONE single-layer short white cape ending above the calves with only one or two large folds.
SIGNATURE WEAPON: silver dragon-head long spear held diagonally in both hands.
COLORS: main white and blue cloth, metal bright silver scale armor, accent sky blue.
FACE: young heroic man, calm determined eyes, clean-shaven.
MOTIF THEME: coiling silver dragon and clouds.
MUST NOT: no brown hair, no multi-layer cape, no loose ribbons everywhere, no helmet covering the ponytail.
```

## 範例：小喬（重做，裁決 07）

```text
CHARACTER: Xiao Qiao (小喬).
SILHOUETTE KEY: twin folding fans held open in both hands, side hair loops with small floral ornaments.
SIGNATURE WEAPON: two painted folding fans with floral pattern.
COLORS: main teal-green, metal light gold, accent red.
FACE: slightly younger and livelier than Da Qiao, bright smile, LIGHT makeup at the same level as the Da Qiao reference.
MOTIF THEME: plum blossoms and flowing water.
MUST NOT: no heavy makeup, no dark eyeshadow, no single feather fan (that is Da Qiao), no pink-white main color.
```

- 角色外框高度佔畫面 90%～96%（頭頂和腳底各留約 3%）。
